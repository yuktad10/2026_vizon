Meet the Orchestra

Before the duet begins, meet the stage it plays on. Every name in this story is measured across the Anglosphere, eight English-speaking countries that make up our orchestra.

🌐 The venue
What is the Anglosphere?

The word was coined by novelist Neal Stephenson in his 1995 book The Diamond Age, a piece of fiction that turned into shorthand for the English-speaking world. It is just 6% of the world's people, but close to 30% of its economy.

Every year, millions of babies are named across it, from New York to New Zealand, London to Auckland. One shared language, eight very different homes. We turned each name into a track and asked one question: which songs travel, and which stay home?

🇮🇪 Our two composers write from one of these chairs, Ireland, a Gaelic voice in the orchestra.
