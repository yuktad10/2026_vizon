Turning Up the Volume
From silent films to TikTok, each media revolution amplified names across borders. The louder the shared signal, the more our playlists sync up.
Did you know? For years, zero babies were named Lucifer. Then Netflix's Lucifer (2016) rebranded the devil as a charming lead, and the name went from mute to a peak of 77. One loud enough signal can move a line no one dared cross.
